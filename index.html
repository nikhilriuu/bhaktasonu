01 / About
Behind the channel

"It's okay if you don't like me — not everyone has the same taste."

I'm Sonu — an entertainer and video creator based in Muzaffarpur, Bihar, posting practical tips almost every day. Bhakta Sonu started as a way to share what I was learning about YouTube and Facebook growth, and it's grown into a small community of people who want straight answers instead of clickbait.

Off camera, I work as an Automobile Service Advisor, so the same habit of diagnosing a problem and fixing it step by step carries into every video — whether that's a growth tip, a setting you didn't know existed, or whether a new gadget is actually worth your money.
